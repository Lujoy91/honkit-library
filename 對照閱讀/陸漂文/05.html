05 喝醉的陸是那種會變得昏昏欲睡、開始發呆的類型…

🇹🇼 中文翻譯

Drunk Luuk being the type to get sleepy and space out and needing to be taken care of by Rover to be brought back into the faculty's accomodations.

喝醉的陸是那種會變得昏昏欲睡、開始發呆的類型，需要漂泊者照顧著才能把他帶回教職人員宿舍。

Rover was going to leave after making sure Luuk was settled in and comfortable for the night—despite his unique circumstances, he's /still/ a student and he had to show up for his classes and maintain a higher percentage of attendance—but then he suddenly hears a quiet, drowsy laughter come from the direction of Luuk's bed.

在確認陸已經安頓好、今晚能舒服休息後，漂泊者原本打算離開了——儘管他有著特殊的境遇，但他「依然」是個學生，得準時去上課並維持一定的出席率——但就在這時，他突然聽到陸的床鋪方向傳來一陣輕柔而帶著睡意的笑聲。

"Looks like I'm dreaming of you again."

「看來我又夢見你了。」

Rover immediately halts in his tracks.

漂泊者頓時停下了腳步。

When he turns back around, Luuk is gazing at him and wearing a face with so much adoration that it silences whatever he was planning to say. There's a golden tint to his cheeks, his equivalent of a blush, and his hair is in a fluffy disarray from being untied and spreading out over his pillow.

當他轉過身時，陸正凝視著他，臉上的神情充滿了深深的眷戀，讓漂泊者原本打算說出口的話全部卡在了喉嚨裡。陸的雙頰泛著淡金色的暈紅——那是屬於他的面紅耳赤——未綁起的金色長發有些蓬亂地散落在枕頭上。

He looks soft.

他看起來是那麼的軟綿柔和。

"...You dream of me?" Rover hears himself ask.

「……你常夢見我嗎？」漂泊者聽到自己這麼問道。

Luuk stares, and stares.

陸只是盯著他看，久久沒有移開視線。

Slowly, his expression falls and he turns to lie flat back down on his back. He lifts an arm to shield his eyes, taking with it the honesty in his eyes. Perhaps the Rover in his dreams also talks back to him because he doesn't seem surprised.

漸漸地，他臉上的神情收斂了下來，轉過身重新平躺回床上。他抬起一隻手臂擋住雙眼，也順帶遮住了眼中那份毫無防備的真誠。或許他夢裡的那個漂泊者也會回應他，因為他看起來一點也不意外。

"I always do," Luuk murmurs.

「我一直都會夢見。」陸喃喃自語。

The sheets rustle as Rover kneels over his side. He's standing there before he knows it, hovering over Luuk like staring down at him would get him to open up and talk.

隨著漂泊者在他身側跪下，床單發出了摩擦的沙沙聲。等漂泊者意識到時，自己已經站在了那裡，俯身居高臨下地看著陸，彷彿只要這樣盯著，就能讓陸敞開心扉多說些什麼。

He's drunk, Rover tries to remind himself. It's overpowered by the growing loudness of his drumming pulse.

他喝醉了，漂泊者試圖這樣提醒自己，但這個念頭很快就被自己胸膛裡那越來越劇烈狂躁的心跳聲所淹沒。

"I'm right here, you know."

「我就在這裡，你知道的。」

This close, he sees the tight press of Luuk's lips. He's not as in control of himself in this state. His lies and misdirection don't come as easily.

距離這麼近，他能看到陸緊繃抿起的雙唇。在這種狀態下的陸無法完美地掌控自己，他的謊言與佯裝不再那麼信手拈來。

Rover can't quite understand why that makes him want to smile.

漂泊者不太明白，為什麼這會讓自己忍不住想笑。

"Just for now," Luuk hoarsely replies. "You're here, just for now."

「只是現在而已，」陸的聲音有些啞。「你只是『現在』在這裡。」

"I'll be here tomorrow, too."

「我明天也會在這裡。」

The bitterness that graces Luuk's lips is unfamiliar. Raw, without anything to hide it, and yet still attractive on him. "I'm not reckless enough to drink for several nights."

陸唇角浮現出的那抹苦澀令人感到陌生。那是未經掩飾、赤裸裸的情緒，可展現在他身上卻依然該死地吸引人。「我還沒不理性到連續喝好幾個晚上的酒。」

"So I only show up in your dreams when you're drunk?" That's... worrying, actually. He's seen the state of Luuk's workspace. This couldn't be the first time.

「所以只有在你喝醉的時候，我才會出現在你的夢裡？」這……其實有點讓人擔心。漂泊者見識過陸工作區的狼藉模樣，這絕對不會是他第一次這麼做。

Luuk stays silent.

陸保持著沉默。

Rover supposes that's his way of saying yes.

漂泊者想，這大概就是他表示認同的方式了。

Letting a smile overtake his features, Rover gently encircles his hand around Luuk's wrist to remove his arm from the upper half of his face. Luuk's eyes had been open, half-lidded and red, stubbornly cast to the side like he'd be able to avoid Rover's 'illusion' if he keeps looking away. Rover leans down until he's sure Luuk can feel the soft, warm puffs of his breath.

漂泊者臉上揚起一抹微笑，輕柔地用手環住陸的手腕，將他擋在半張臉上的手臂移開。陸的眼睛一直睜著，半闔的紅睦固執地撇向一旁，彷彿只要不看漂泊者，就能無視這個他以為的「幻覺」。漂泊者俯下身，直到確定陸能感受到自己吐出的輕柔溫熱呼吸。

"How long do you plan on looking away?" Rover murmurs. Even to his own ears, he sounds painfully fond. If he had a mirror right now, would he be wearing the same face that Luuk made when he was looking at him earlier? "I'm right here. Can't you feel me touching you?"

「你打算躲開視線多久？」漂泊者低語道。就連他自己聽起來，語氣都寵溺得令人發疼。如果現在有一面鏡子，他臉上的神情是不是也和剛才陸看著他時一模一樣？「我就在這裡。你感受不到我在摸你嗎？」

Between the silken strands of his golden hair, Rover catches the darkening bronze in the tip of Luuk's ears. He laughs, tightening his grasp around his wrist and playfully nosing at the shell of his ear.

在陸那如絲綢般的金髮間，漂泊者捕捉到了他耳尖漸漸加深的古銅色。他笑了出來，握著手腕的手微微收緊，惡作劇般地用鼻子貼了貼陸的耳廓。

"C'mon," he says, still in that same, quiet and affectionate tone. It's sweet enough to make his own cheeks hurt from smiling. "Luuk, I know you've sobered up."

「好啦，」他依然用那種溫柔又寵溺的語氣說著，甜得連他自己的雙頰都因為微笑而泛酸。「陸，我知道你已經醒酒了。」

That gets him the reaction he wants.

這句話終於得到了他想要的反應。

A bright-red eye flicks toward him through the curtain of his blond hair. He looks slightly petulant, Rover thinks with an amused huff. Still not fully sober, then. Enough to recognize Rover, it seems like, but not enough to restrain his now naked desire to be spoiled with Rover's attention.

一雙明亮的紅瞳穿過金黃色的髮絲斜斜地瞟向他。漂泊者好笑地哼了一聲，覺得陸現在看起來有些賭氣。看來還沒完全醒酒呢——雖然足夠認出漂泊者，但還不足以壓抑他此刻赤裸裸、渴望被漂泊者關注與嬌慣的慾望。

He's been waiting a while. If anything, it's a surprise Luuk hadn't said or done more to get Rover to pay attention to him.

他已經等了很久了。真要說的話，陸居然沒有做出更多舉動來吸引漂泊者的注意，反倒讓人有些驚訝。

Rover leans in to rest his forehead against his. "What?" He laughs. "Still don't believe me?"

漂泊者湊得更近，將自己的額頭抵上了陸的額頭。「怎麼了？」他笑著問。「還是不相信我？」

"You were leaving."

「你剛剛準備要走了。」

"A student can't be seen around here for too long. You know that."

「學生可不能在這種地方待太久，這你是知道的。」

"...Stay a little longer?"

「……再多陪我一會兒，好嗎？」

"I'll stay until you fall asleep," he promises, smiling gently down at Luuk.

「我會陪著你，直到你睡著為止。」漂泊者對著陸溫柔地微笑，許下了承諾。
