漂泊者在陸的吻中艱難地喘著氣，當他感覺到陸貼著自己的嘴唇、因為自己處理這種程度的肢體親密時顯得極其生疏而忍不住笑出來時，他微微噘起了嘴。

The desperate fist that Rover has twisted into his coat and the adorably confused outward loll of his tongue when Luuk leaves to let him breathe tells him he hadn't done too much of a terrible job.

"...What was that for?" Rover pants.

「......剛剛那是幹嘛？」漂泊者喘著氣問。

Luuk gently holds his cheek. "Just wanted to kiss you. You didn't like it?"

陸溫柔地托住他的臉頰。「只是想親你而已。你不喜歡嗎？」

Rover's expression twists with a familiar stubbornness that makes his eyebrows furrow and the corners of his lips curl down. One of Luuk's many favorite faces to see on him, because that usually meant—

『看來這就是他的答案了，』陸邊想，邊配合地張開嘴，給了他一個深情的熱吻。
