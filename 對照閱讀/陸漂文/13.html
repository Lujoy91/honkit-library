When confronted with a sweets shop offering a couple's discount, Luuk would politely offer to cover the cost of two individual orders. Rover, however, has a different idea.

當兩人來到一家提供情侶優惠的甜點店前，陸禮貌地打算買單兩份單人套餐。然而，漂泊者卻有不同的想法。

"Will you two be sharing our 'Sweetheart deal' today?"

「兩位今天是要合點我們的『甜蜜情人套餐』嗎？」

Luuk's smile takes an uncomfortable twist. "No, it's for—"

陸嘴角的微笑有些僵硬歪斜。「不，我們是要——」

"Yes," Rover interrupts. At the cashier's skeptical back-and-forth glance between the two of them, he plays up the act, sliding his palm into place against Luuk's and leaning his head on his shoulder. "Sorry, he gets shy about us. We'll share."

「是的，」漂泊者打斷了他。面對收銀員在他們兩人之間來回狐疑盤查的眼神，他索性將戲演得更足，順勢將掌心貼進陸的手掌中，並把頭依偎在陸的肩膀上。「抱歉，他對於我們倆的關係比較害羞。我們要一起吃這份套餐。」

Rover rubs Luuk's fingers in apology. Almost immediately, he feels Luuk relax.

漂泊者輕輕摩挲著陸的手指以示抱歉。幾乎是瞬間，他就感受到陸整個身體放鬆了下來。

"Are you sure?" The beauty of having Luuk play along is that the softness in his voice is wholly genuine. He was probably thinking of placing two separate orders to avoid putting Rover in an uncomfortable position in the eyes of others.

「你確定嗎？」讓陸配合演出最棒的一點，就是他聲音裡的那份溫柔絕對是發自內心的真誠。陸剛才大概是想點兩份獨立套餐，好避免讓漂泊者在旁人眼裡陷入尷尬的處境。

Neither of them are short on shell credits, so it's a non-problem, truthfully speaking.

說實話，他們兩個都不缺貝幣，所以這根本不是錢的問題。

But it's not often that they get the chance to spend time together. If there's an opportunity for him to make this a memorable time for Luuk, then he'd take it.

但他們能聚在一起的時間並不常有。如果能有機會讓這段時光成為陸心中難忘的回憶，漂泊者絕對會好好把握。

It's surprisingly easy to lift their intertwined hands so that he could place an affectionate kiss over the back of Luuk's knuckles. The muscles slightly jump under the press of his lips and he tightens his hold as he pulls his head back to gaze up at Luuk.

漂泊者順手提起他們交纏的手掌，將一個充滿愛意的吻落在了陸的指節背上——這動作順暢得令人驚訝。感受著陸的肌肉在嘴唇按壓下微微跳動，漂泊者收緊了握持，隨後昂起頭仰望著陸。

"Don't worry," Rover says, smiling right into the back of his hand as he reads Luuk's thoughts through the visible cracks in his composure. That wasn't for show. He simply felt like doing it, that's all. "I'm sure."

「別擔心，」漂泊者隔著陸的手背對他微笑著說道，透過陸那完美鎮定中露出的絲絲縫隙，讀懂了他的心思。剛剛那個吻並非演戲，他只是純粹想這麼做罷了，僅此而已。「我很確定。」

There is a question in the sweetening of those red eyes looking down at him, and Rover patiently smiles, waiting as his answer.

那雙俯視著他的紅眸漸漸染上甜意，眼神裡帶著一絲無聲的詢問，而漂泊者只是耐心微笑著，將這份微笑作為回答。

When Luuk leans down, he welcomes the gentle press of his lips on the top of his head.

當陸俯下身時，漂泊者欣然接受了他落在大腦門上輕柔的一吻。

"...Okay?" Luuk murmurs.

「......這樣真的可以嗎？」陸輕聲喃喃道。

Rover chuckles, leaning into it. Trust in him to ask in all the ways he knows how just so he could make sure he isn't doing anything Rover doesn't like. "Yes, it's okay," he replies.

漂泊者低聲笑了起來，舒服地往他懷裡靠了靠。陸就是這樣，總是會用盡他所知道的一切方式確認，只為了確保自己沒有做出任何漂泊者不喜歡的事。「嗯，真的可以，」他回答道。
